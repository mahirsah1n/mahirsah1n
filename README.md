## about

I'm Mahir. Turkish, living in Kraków, studying Computer Science at the Krakow University of Economics.

Most things I know I learned twice — once badly, then again properly. I'm slow on purpose. I'd rather understand one thing all the way down than ship five I can't explain out loud.

Right now that means Python, backend work, and the math sitting underneath machine learning. Away from the screen it's football with a group of international students, the gym, and a stack of books I keep promising myself I'll finish this month.

The notebook on the desk isn't decoration. Most of the thinking happens there first.

## things I've built

**[ACL Rehabilitation Tracker](https://github.com/mahirsah1n/ACL_REHAB_TRACKER)** — `python`
I tore my ACL and ended up tracking exercises, food, and pain across three separate notebooks. That got unmanageable, so I wrote a CLI to do it. Also my CS50 final project.

**[UEK Schedule Scraper](https://github.com/mahirsah1n/UEK_SCHEDULE_SCRAPER)** — `python · selenium · beautifulsoup`
Logs into USOSweb, pulls my class schedule, exports a `.ics`. Syncs straight into Google or Apple Calendar. Written because checking the portal every morning was annoying.

**[CeneoFlask](https://github.com/mahirsah1n/CeneoFlask)** — `python · flask`
Scrapes product reviews off Ceneo.pl and turns them into stats and charts you can actually read.
